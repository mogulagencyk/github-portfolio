Where business needs meet technical execution.

I am a technology professional and graduating Computer Information Science student at Lamar University, with an interdisciplinary background in business, information systems, and project delivery. My work focuses on translating complex business needs into organized, actionable work for technical teams.

In professional environments, I have supported Agile delivery, planning, testing coordination, stakeholder collaboration, and cross-functional communication. I have experience working with enterprise technology initiatives, including SAP-related business processes, as well as tools and practices such as Azure DevOps, Jira, documentation, backlog management, and iterative delivery. These experiences have strengthened my ability to understand both how technology works and why organizations need it.

Alongside enterprise work, I have developed and managed digital brand and website projects. Mogul Agency, Shop Mogul, and Moguls Academy represent different types of user experiences: a services-oriented presence, an e-commerce storefront, and an education-focused platform. I enjoy connecting audience needs, information architecture, functionality, and business objectives.

My academic interests include computer networks, cybersecurity, software development, and artificial intelligence. Coursework has given me opportunities to investigate topics such as convolutional neural networks for image classification and TCP congestion-control behavior. I value hands-on projects because they move a concept from theory into something that can be demonstrated, measured, and improved.

As I graduate in December 2026, my professional goal is to progress in roles that bridge technical teams and business strategy, including business analysis, technical project management, product ownership, and enterprise systems delivery. I aim to contribute through clear communication, evidence-based decisions, well-documented work, and thoughtful problem-solving. This portfolio brings together selected digital projects, academic work, and a small software application to show that perspective in practice.
